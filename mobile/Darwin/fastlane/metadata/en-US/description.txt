LocalSR makes small, blurry and blocky photos sharper, right on your iPhone.

Pick a photo, or share it to LocalSR from Photos or any other app. LocalSR enlarges it up to 4× and cleans up blur and JPEG blocks, then saves a new photo next to the original. Your original isn't changed.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• No account, no sign-up, no uploads. LocalSR works offline.
• The models run on the Neural Engine. On first use, LocalSR checks which chip of your iPhone gives correct results fastest and uses it.
• A 12 MP photo takes about 2 seconds on an iPhone 17 Pro and about 6 seconds on an iPhone 11 Pro.

WHAT YOU GET
• Up to 3 MP: 4×. Up to 12 MP: 2×, or 4× with Full 4× in Settings. Larger photos are reduced to 12 MP first.
• Two models: Best for the most detail, Fast for many photos at once.
• Compare before and after with a slider and zoom in to check the details.
• "Upscale with LocalSR" in the share sheet, for one photo or many.
• Keeps the date and location of the original.

HONEST ABOUT WHAT IT DOES
LocalSR sharpens what is in the photo. It can add fine texture, but it can't bring back detail that isn't there: text that can't be read in the photo stays unreadable.
