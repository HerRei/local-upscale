LocalSR makes small, blurry and blocky photos sharper, right on your phone.

Pick a photo, or share it to LocalSR from your gallery or any other app. LocalSR enlarges it up to 4× and cleans up blur and JPEG blocks, then saves a new photo to Pictures/LocalSR. Your original isn't changed.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• No account, no sign-up, no uploads. LocalSR has no internet permission.
• On first use, LocalSR checks which chip of your phone (GPU or CPU, or the NPU where the phone provides one) gives correct results fastest and uses it.

WHAT YOU GET
• Up to 3 MP: 4×. Up to 12 MP: 2×. Larger photos are reduced to 12 MP first.
• Two models: Best for the most detail, Fast for many photos at once.
• Compare before and after with a slider and zoom in to check the details.
• "Upscale with LocalSR" in the share menu, for one photo or many, with a progress notification while it works.

HONEST ABOUT WHAT IT DOES
LocalSR sharpens what is in the photo. It can add fine texture, but it can't bring back detail that isn't there: text that can't be read in the photo stays unreadable.
