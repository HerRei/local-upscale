# LocalSR v0.2.1-beta

Comparison controls that stay out of the way, and a divider that stays put
when you zoom in.

## What changed

**The divider stays where it is.** Zooming and panning used to move the
comparison divider with the picture, so a close look at a corner pushed it off
screen and showed only one side. It now keeps its place, its 2 px line and its
handle at every zoom; both images move underneath it, so at 1000 % you still see
the original on the left and the result on the right. Fit keeps the divider
where you put it.

**Original · Split · Enhanced.** The two labels above the preview are now a
quiet switch: Original shows the whole original, Split the divider, Enhanced the
whole result. Your choice stays until the next result, which starts in Split.
The same three views are in the View menu. Hold **M** to show the original from
any view; let go to return. Images and videos work the same way.

**Zoom controls only when you need them.** The zoom box moved from the top right
of the picture to the bottom right and appears while the pointer is over the
image. Scrolling, pinching and View › Fit to Window and Actual Pixels work as
before.

## Download

[Download page](https://herrei.github.io/localsr/download/). The Mac mini
serves the files through the Infomaniak VPS; the attached GitHub files are
mirrors.

- `LocalSR-v0.2.1-beta-macOS-arm64.dmg` — Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later.
  Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Installed macOS betas receive
  this version through the app's update check; the update includes the
  inference engine.
- `LocalSR-v0.2.1-beta-Windows-CPU-x86_64.exe` — Windows 10 or 11, x86-64.
  Unsigned, so SmartScreen asks first. DirectML and NVIDIA CUDA editions are
  attached as well and remain untested on those GPUs.
- `LocalSR-v0.2.1-beta-Linux-CPU-x86_64.AppImage` — x86-64, glibc 2.39 or
  later. AMD ROCm and NVIDIA CUDA editions come in parts.
- `*-source-and-notices.tar.gz` — source, build instructions and third-party
  notices for each build.

File checksums are in `SHA256SUMS` and the `.sha256` files.

## Validation

The comparison changes were checked in the interface tests (184 pass) and in
the running app on a 16 GB M1 Pro: zooming from Fit to over 1000 % and panning
left the divider in the same screen position with the same line and handle,
holding M and the three views showed the expected image, and the zoom controls
appeared only over the picture. The packaged Mac app, its engine and every
Windows and Linux package went through the same automated build checks as
v0.2.0-beta.

## Known limitations

Editing, SeedVR2, HDR preservation, de-flicker and video face processing remain
experimental. See [KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](https://github.com/HerRei/local-upscale/blob/main/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md)
for the current list.

Report problems on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/HerRei/local-upscale/issues)
with your app version and steps to reproduce.
