# LocalSR v0.1.5-beta

Installing LocalSR on a Mac is now a single drag.

## Download

- `LocalSR-v0.1.5-beta-macOS-arm64.dmg` — Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later.
  Signed and notarized; uses the Metal (MPS) backend.
- `LocalSR-v0.1.5-beta-source-and-notices.tar.gz` — source, build
  instructions and third-party license notices for exactly that build.

Every file's SHA-256 is in `SHA256SUMS` and in the `.sha256` beside it.

Windows and Linux stay on
[v0.1.3-beta](https://github.com/HerRei/local-upscale/releases/tag/v0.1.3-beta)
for now.

Installed macOS betas update themselves: the app checks the feed at launch,
verifies the download's size, SHA-256 and signature, backs up settings, recipes
and the queue, then installs and restarts. This update also replaces the
inference engine, so it downloads about 760 MB.

## What's new

- **A proper install window.** Opening the disk image shows one window with
  LocalSR on the left, your Applications folder on the right and an arrow
  between them: drag the icon across and you are done. The disk itself shows
  the LocalSR icon.
- **A centred icon.** The terracotta pixel in the app icon and the in-app logo
  sat slightly right of and below the middle of its crop marks; it is now
  exactly centred.

Nothing changed in upscaling, restoration or editing; models and settings carry
over unchanged.

## Known limitations

- Edit is still **experimental**. Its memory profiles are estimates and no
  real edit has been validated on the 16 GB M1 Pro used for development yet.
  Close other apps before editing.
- Edits are 512, 768 or 1024 px on the longest side, one image at a time.
- HDR preservation, SeedVR2 3B, de-flicker and video face processing are Labs
  features with unverified perceptual quality.
- The two HAT face companions still require a manual import.

The full list is in [KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](https://github.com/HerRei/local-upscale/blob/main/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).

Report problems on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/HerRei/local-upscale/issues)
