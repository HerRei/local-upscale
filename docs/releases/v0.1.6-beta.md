# LocalSR v0.1.6-beta

Edit now runs on 16 GB Macs, and LocalSR opens HEIC photos and camera RAW files.

## Download

- `LocalSR-v0.1.6-beta-macOS-arm64.dmg` — Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later.
  Signed and notarized; uses the Metal (MPS) backend.
- `LocalSR-v0.1.6-beta-source-and-notices.tar.gz` — source, build
  instructions and third-party license notices for exactly that build.

Every file's SHA-256 is in `SHA256SUMS` and in the `.sha256` beside it.

Windows and Linux stay on
[v0.1.3-beta](https://github.com/HerRei/local-upscale/releases/tag/v0.1.3-beta)
for now.

Installed macOS betas update themselves: the app checks the feed at launch,
verifies the download's size, SHA-256 and signature, backs up settings, recipes
and the queue, then installs and restarts. This update also replaces the
inference engine, so it downloads about 760 MB.

## What's new

- **Edit works on 16 GB Macs.** The memory guard misread how much memory a
  Mac has free: it counted only free and inactive pages and kept 4 GB in
  reserve, so it refused FLUX.2 klein 4B on a 16 GB MacBook Pro that macOS
  itself reported as 70% free. It now asks macOS, keeps 2 GB in reserve, and
  watches the editor's real memory use. On that Mac, with other apps open, an
  edit at 512 px takes about two minutes and does not swap.
- **More photo formats.** HEIC and HEIF from iPhones and cameras, and JPEG XL,
  open on a Mac through macOS's own decoders. AVIF, JPEG 2000 and GIF open too,
  and camera RAW files from Canon (CR2/CR3), Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, Olympus,
  Panasonic, Pentax and others, not only DNG.
- **Progress you can see.** An edit names what it is doing (reading the photo,
  reading your instruction, step 2 of 4, finishing the image) with a moving
  progress bar on the photo and in the status bar.
- **The right model by default.** A Mac starts with the best editing model that
  leaves most of its memory to macOS and your apps: FLUX.2 klein Q4_0 on 16 GB,
  Q8_0 on 24–32 GB, Qwen Image Edit from 36 GB. A model you chose is kept.
- **A calmer memory guard.** It lets macOS compress memory and swap a little,
  and stops an edit only when the Mac is really short (critical pressure or
  heavy swapping), saying why.
- **Fixed:** installing a newer disk image over a copy that had updated itself
  kept the older inference engine.
- From v0.1.5-beta: the disk image opens as a drag-to-Applications window, and
  the dot in the icon is centred.

## Known limitations

- Edit is still **experimental**. FLUX.2 klein 4B Q4_0 at 512 px is validated
  on a 16 GB M1 Pro; FLUX.2 klein Q8_0, the Qwen bundles and larger edit sizes
  have not run on real hardware yet.
- Edits are 512, 768 or 1024 px on the longest side, one image at a time.
- HDR preservation, SeedVR2 3B, de-flicker and video face processing are Labs
  features with unverified perceptual quality.
- The two HAT face companions still require a manual import.

The full list is in [KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](https://github.com/HerRei/local-upscale/blob/main/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).
