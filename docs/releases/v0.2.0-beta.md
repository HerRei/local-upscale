# LocalSR v0.2.0-beta

A calmer, simpler window, redesigned for people who open LocalSR for the first
time.

## Why

Watching new users, the same things got in the way: two or three places offered
to download a model, presets were named after model codenames, a top bar
repeated the app name next to buttons that already lived in the menu bar, and
blue, green and amber text competed with the photo. This release keeps what
LocalSR does and changes how it asks you to do it.

## What changed

**One way to start.** Add a photo, pick Quick or Best, press the button at the
bottom right. If the model is not on your computer yet, the button says so
("Download 4 MB, then upscale") and does both. Per-model download buttons, the
first-run download notice and the license box with its own download button are
gone; a model's license is now a one-line credit with links to its source and
terms.

**Plain choices.** Quick and Best are described by what you get. Upscale,
Restore and Edit form one switch with a single caption, and Upscale handles
videos too, so the separate Upscale Video task is gone. Models that are ready
carry no label; the plan only mentions a download when one is needed. A line
beside the button shows what the next job will make, for example
`2268 × 4032 → 9072 × 16128 · 4× · Apple GPU`.

**No top bar.** On wide windows the pane headings form the title row, with the
window buttons beside Media. Models opens from **View › Models…** (⇧⌘M) or the
**Change…** link, Performance and Diagnostics from the View menu, and **Check
for Updates…** from the LocalSR menu (Help on Windows and Linux). Narrow windows
keep a slim bar to switch between Media, Preview and Enhance.

**A quieter look.** The app now ships IBM Plex Sans, so Mac, Windows and Linux
show the same typeface. Corners are square, panels are neutral grey so you judge
colour against a neutral surround, and colour appears in one place: the button
that starts work and its progress, in the terracotta of the logo. Until there is
something to start, the window has no colour at all.

## Fixed

- New installations start on the GPU (Apple silicon, CUDA or ROCm). Until now a
  fresh install defaulted to the CPU, so the first upscale on a Mac ran several
  times slower than necessary until Hardware was changed under Advanced.
- Choosing Best no longer shows two download buttons for one file, with two
  different sizes.

## Download

[Download for macOS](https://downloads.hermesr.duckdns.org/releases/v0.2.0-beta/LocalSR-v0.2.0-beta-macOS-arm64.dmg).
The Mac mini serves the files through the Infomaniak VPS. The attached GitHub
files are mirrors.

- `LocalSR-v0.2.0-beta-macOS-arm64.dmg` — Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later.
  Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.
- `LocalSR-v0.2.0-beta-source-and-notices.tar.gz` — source, build instructions
  and third-party notices for this build.

Installed macOS betas receive this version through the app's update check.
The update includes the inference engine. File checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.

## Known limitations

Editing, SeedVR2, HDR preservation, de-flicker and video face processing remain
experimental. See [KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](https://github.com/HerRei/local-upscale/blob/main/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md)
for the current list.

Report problems on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/HerRei/local-upscale/issues)
with your app version and steps to reproduce.
