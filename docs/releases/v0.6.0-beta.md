# LocalSR v0.6.0-beta

## Which file do I need?

| Computer | Download |
|---|---|
| Mac (M1 or newer) | `LocalSR-v0.6.0-beta-macOS-arm64.dmg` |
| Windows | `LocalSR-v0.6.0-beta-Windows-CPU-x86_64.exe` |
| Linux | `LocalSR-v0.6.0-beta-Linux-CPU-x86_64.AppImage`, or `LocalSR-v0.6.0-beta-Linux-CPU-amd64.deb` on Ubuntu and Debian |

The app offers the faster GPU engine after setup.

## Offline and advanced downloads

| Computer | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows, AMD or Intel graphics | `LocalSR-v0.6.0-beta-Windows-DirectML-x86_64.exe` |
| Windows, NVIDIA | `LocalSR-v0.6.0-beta-Windows-CUDA-x86_64.exe` and all `LocalSR-v0.6.0-beta-Windows-CUDA-x86_64.engine.tar.gz.part-*` files in the same folder |
| Linux, AMD | all `LocalSR-v0.6.0-beta-Linux-ROCm-x86_64.AppImage.part-*` files, joined |
| Linux, NVIDIA | all `LocalSR-v0.6.0-beta-Linux-CUDA-x86_64.AppImage.part-*` files, joined |

To import an engine offline, download the `*.engine-payload.json` manifest, its `.engine.tar.gz.part-*` files and their `.sig` signature files into one folder, then open **Advanced -> Engine -> Change… -> Import engine from file…** and choose the `*.engine-payload.json` file.

The other files (signatures, checksums and source archives) are for verification and updates.

## What changed

- **LocalSR's own models.** Quick and Best for photos are now LocalSR's own x4 upscalers
  (4xLocalSRPhoto_PULSE-64x8 and 4xLocalSRPhoto_PULSE-96x12, weights CC BY 4.0), trained only on public-domain (CC0)
  photos and running fully on the Neural Engine. On a sealed test set of 99 photos Quick
  beats the SPAN model it replaces on every measure at the same speed; Best matches
  NomosWebPhoto on detail with 2.6 dB more fidelity and runs about four times as fast
  (27 s instead of 99 s for a 12 MP photo on an M1 Pro). Both models, their numbers and the
  technical report are at github.com/HerRei/localsr-models. The previous models stay in the
  model library; Illustration presets are unchanged.
- **First launch.** A two-page setup: first privacy (update checks, the anonymous count and
  crash reports, each with a plain explanation and a link to what LocalSR sends and why),
  then the look of the app, the menu bar or tray icon and a sound when a file finishes.
  Nothing is sent before Done, and everything can be changed later in Settings.
- **Settings.** One Settings window (⌘, on a Mac; File > Settings… elsewhere) holds every
  app-wide choice: appearance, default hardware, low priority, notifications and sound,
  the menu bar icon, the model folder, caches, External FFmpeg, updates and crash
  reports. Settings holds the defaults: Advanced chooses the hardware and low priority
  for this session only, and low priority covers the running job and the whole queue.
  After a GPU failure, Retry on CPU runs that job on the CPU without changing the
  default.
- **High contrast.** Settings > Appearance > High contrast gives the dark and the light
  theme stronger text, borders and focus outlines. It also turns on when the computer
  asks for more contrast.
- **Viewer background.** The picture can sit on a checkerboard, black, neutral grey or
  white: Settings > Appearance, or the swatch beside the zoom controls. Preview, Compare
  and video use it.
- **Upscale & restore.** Restore is no longer a separate task: the Size row (Same size,
  2×, 3×, 4×) decides whether the picture gets bigger, and the Fix chips (Noise, Blur,
  JPEG artifacts, Colour) work at any size. Quick and Best choose the fix models as well,
  a fast one or the most careful one. Saved recipes and settings keep working.
- **Video models in the model list.** SeedVR2 and FlashVSR are chosen like any other model,
  from the model row and the library, with quality and speed ratings. A "?" explains frame
  by frame against several frames at once, and models that cannot run on this computer
  say why.
- **Colour for video.** Black-and-white videos can be coloured with the Colour fix, at
  any size. Colours are carried from frame to frame, so they stay steady. A video
  that already has colour skips the step, and a Colour choice made for photos is not
  carried over to videos (#164).
- **Updates from 0.4.0 and 0.5.0.** They refused the update to 0.5.2 ("unknown field
  `arch`"). The update files are again in a form they read, an engine they install is
  taken over, and every release is now checked against the updaters of older
  releases before it ships.
- **Stopping a job.** Cancel asks whether to keep the finished part or discard it;
  Settings can make it always keep or always discard. A video keeps its finished
  frames (frame-by-frame and SeedVR), a photo its finished tiles with the rest left
  as in the original, also in a queue. Kept parts are marked Unfinished: a photo shows
  its finished tiles on hover, and comparing an unfinished video steps through its
  frames against the same frames of the original.
- **De-flicker.** A new engine for every frame-by-frame model: it evens out brightness
  flicker in the original video and the shimmer that upscaling adds in still areas,
  decided from the original so motion, cuts and real light changes pass through. It
  removes more shimmer than before at a fraction of the cost. Still off by default.
- **FlashVSR v1.1 · Labs.** A one-step diffusion video upscaler for Apple silicon Macs
  with 16 GB or more (MLX), AMD ROCm on Linux and NVIDIA CUDA on Linux and Windows with
  12 GB or more VRAM. It works on 37 frames at a time with 8 frames of run-up, splits
  large frames into tiles sized to the available memory, and follows the output resolution
  you choose (up to 4× directly, larger sizes are resized). The weights (6.4 GB,
  Apache-2.0) download on first use. CUDA has not been run on NVIDIA hardware yet.
- **Compare.** Choose which side's audio plays, step frame by frame, and the details of
  each side stay within a fixed height however long the model chain is.
- **Menu bar.** An icon in the menu bar or system tray shows what is running, how many
  files wait and the progress, with Pause and Resume, and a notification for each
  finished file. New installs choose it in the first-launch setup; on a Mac the icon
  can be monochrome or in colour (#112).
- **Compare results.** Each side of a comparison lists how it was made: models and
  fixes in order, scale, size, time, device and date (#145).
- **Your own models.** Own checkpoints get a name you can change and their own group
  in the model picker. A .pth, .pt or .ckpt file is confirmed once per file, not per
  job. NERVE and HEART checkpoints load too (#146, #154).
- **Illustration Best.** Best for illustrations now uses ESRGAN ×4 HFA2k, which keeps
  flat colours, white backgrounds, QR codes and fine lines. RealPLKSR HFA2k stays in
  the model library (#122).
- **Heavy video jobs.** Frame interpolation after upscaling needs about half the
  graphics memory. When a frame pair still does not fit, the other models wait in
  system memory meanwhile, so very large videos stay on the GPU; only as a last
  resort does it run on the CPU, and the result says so. Running out of graphics
  memory reads as a plain sentence with what to change.
- **Progress.** The status line names the step that runs (for example Adding colour
  or De-flickering, with a count), downloads share one progress bar, and Saving the result appears only
  when the file is being written. Interpolation can be moved before or after
  upscaling from the plan list.
- **Cache.** Settings show the size of the compiled model caches with Clear cache,
  which asks first;
  unused entries are removed automatically unless you turn that off (#126).
- **Settings from a newer version.** LocalSR keeps the file untouched, runs with
  defaults and says so, and an update to that newer version is no longer blocked
  (#123).
- **GPU engines.** Program files are checked at every start, a reinstall keeps the
  working copy until the new one has started, and linked engine folders are refused
  (#159).
- **Windows installer.** Offers Just me (default, no administrator rights) or Everyone
  on this computer.
- **Debian package.** The CPU edition for Ubuntu 22.04+ and Debian 12+ as a .deb that
  `apt` installs into the applications menu and removes with `apt remove`. It reports new
  versions instead of updating itself, and downloads the CUDA or ROCm engine inside the
  app. The AppImage stays (#165).
- **Fixes.** Comparing videos no longer stutters, stops where an unfinished part ends and
  keeps its position and audio choice when you switch between Slider and Boxes; while
  playing in the Mac app the two sides can still be a few frames apart, and paused or
  stepped frames always match. The live preview of Colour plus upscaling stays in colour. Stopping a large
  photo with "keep the finished part" gives it the time to save instead of restarting the
  engine after a few seconds, and kept parts always appear in Outputs. LocalSR's own
  OpenCV now includes optical flow, which de-flicker and video colour need.
- **Fixes.** The video encoder is always flushed before it closes (#115). Release
  files can be attached to a draft release, and signing needs less disk space (#114).
